Assumptions, input record and scientific basis What this estimate can and cannot tell us

Assumptions: stationary object; symmetric, horizontal opposing normal forces; identical dry-contact friction; no torque or off-centre load; an established grasp; Coulomb friction. The estimate does not model crushing, scratching, movement, or gripper actuator capability.

The uncertainty calculation is valid only within the bounds you supplied. Even an estimate below a supplied force limit is not a physically validated grip.
